# Governance reference

Use structured messages and the append-only ledger. Councils are odd-sized with at least three eligible specialist agents. Cast votes independently before revealing rationales. Require more than half of all eligible voters for a majority. Abstentions do not lower quorum.

Security, data loss, compliance, failed tests, missing requirement coverage, changed artifacts, missing provenance, and human approval are hard gates. When no majority exists, remove disqualified options and maximize the worst normalized utility across predeclared risk dimensions. Ties and incomparable evidence require human review. Preserve every vote, rationale, conflict, source, model identity, outcome, and supersession.
